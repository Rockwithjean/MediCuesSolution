# 📧 Medi Cues Solution — Contact Form Email Guide

**For:** The person managing enquiries at `medicuesltd@gmail.com`
**Website:** Medi Cues Solution

---

## What Is This?

The Medi Cues Solution website has a **Contact Form** on the Contact page. When a visitor fills out and submits the form, their message is automatically sent to the company email: **medicuesltd@gmail.com**.

You do not need any special software or dashboard. Everything arrives directly in Gmail.

---

## ⚠️ One-Time Setup (Very Important — Do This First)

Before the form can deliver emails, the Gmail account needs to be verified **once**.

Here is what to do:

1. The first time someone submits the contact form, **FormSubmit** (the service that powers the form) will send an automatic email to `medicuesltd@gmail.com`.
2. Open that email — the subject will be something like **"Confirm your FormSubmit"**.
3. Click the **"Confirm your email address"** button or link inside the email.
4. You will see a confirmation page. That means setup is complete.

> ✅ You only need to do this **one time ever**. After that, the form works automatically for all future submissions.

---

## 📩 What Happens When Someone Sends a Message

When a visitor submits the contact form on the website:

1. You will receive an email at **medicuesltd@gmail.com**
2. The email will contain:
   - The visitor's **Full Name**
   - Their **Email Address**
   - Their **Phone Number** (if provided)
   - The **Subject** they selected (e.g. "Medical Equipment Enquiry")
   - Their **Message**

The email subject line will always say:
> **"New Enquiry — Medi Cues Solution Website"**

This makes it easy to identify website enquiries in your inbox.

---

## 💬 How to Respond

To reply to a website enquiry:

1. Open the email you received in Gmail
2. Click **Reply**
3. Your reply will go directly to the visitor's email address
4. Type your response and send as you normally would

There is nothing else to do — no extra steps, no special system.

---

## 🗂️ Recommended Gmail Tips

To stay organised, consider doing the following in Gmail:

- **Create a label** called "Website Enquiries" and filter emails with the subject *"New Enquiry — Medi Cues Solution Website"* to apply that label automatically.
- **Mark as read** once you have responded so you know which ones have been handled.
- **Check your Spam folder** occasionally — sometimes automated emails can be filtered there by mistake.

---

## ❓ What If You Stop Receiving Emails?

If website form submissions stop arriving in your inbox, try the following:

1. Check your **Spam / Junk** folder in Gmail
2. Ask someone to test the contact form and wait a few minutes
3. If still not working, contact the website developer for assistance
